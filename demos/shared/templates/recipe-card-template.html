Bologna, the long way round

From the kitchen of Nonna Adriana

A Bologna Food Walk

Serves: 2 hungry walkers · Time: 3 hrs

Ingredients

    Method

    1. Start early, before the arcades fill up.
    2. Walk slowly. Never pass a shop without looking in.
    3. Taste one thing at each stop, no more.
    4. Keep to the shade of the porticoes if it is hot.
    5. Finish in the piazza and sit a while.
